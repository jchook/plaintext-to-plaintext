# Discop: Provably Secure Steganography in Practice Based on Distribution Copies

IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy 2023; author-hosted PDF. Reviewed 2026-10-18.

An important distribution-preserving baseline using copies of the token distribution. Its recursive variant improves capacity and appears in many later comparisons.

Requires synchronized sampling and inference. A secure token-level sampler alone does not resolve ambiguous detokenization or message framing.
