# Meteor: Cryptographically Secure Steganography for Realistic Distributions

ACM CCS 2021; author-hosted PDF. Reviewed 2026-10-18.

Establishes cryptographic treatment of generative steganography when token entropy varies widely. Relevant to shared randomness, security definitions, and why ordinary arithmetic coding needs careful analysis. Includes historical mobile measurements.

Its guarantee is relative to the agreed generative model. Historical mobile experiments do not validate our current quantized 3B model or phone-to-desktop interoperability.
