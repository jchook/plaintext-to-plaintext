# Additional leads from the research pass

These leads appeared during discovery but were not used to support the main
recommendations. They are retained so that unresolved searches are not mistaken
for reviewed evidence. The linked works are not included as downloaded papers
unless they have their own entry in the archive index.

- [Asymmetric Resource Constrained Decoding for Lightweight Linguistic
  Steganography in Causal LLMs](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-37218-5_7):
  potentially relevant to a lightweight receiver. Search metadata identifies an
  ARES workshop contribution; indexing differed on publication year. Full text
  and practical guarantees were not established during the review.
- [Rank-Token Mapping Steganography in LLM Chatbots: Evaluating Capacity and
  Performance Across Multiple LLMs](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/15/18/4251):
  a September 2026 result found in the date sweep. Its complete evaluation was
  not reviewed, so no capacity or security claim from it is included here.
- [Adaptive dynamic sampling artifact](https://zenodo.org/records/18152848):
  the publication points to implementation materials, but the artifact page
  failed to load during the research pass. The paper itself is archived under
  [adaptive dynamic sampling](2026-adaptive-dynamic-sampling/relevance.md).
- The [curated paper collection](reference-paper-collection/relevance.md)
  contains further asymmetric, rank-based, and compression approaches, including
  DualSteg, CARTS, and SemanticZip. Those entries require primary-source review
  before they can support comparisons with our POC.

The possible proof issue flagged in the list-decoding preprint is recorded with
its uncertainty in [that source's note](2026-list-decoding/relevance.md). It is a
review lead, not an established correction to the paper.
