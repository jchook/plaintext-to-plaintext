# Provably Secure Generative Linguistic Steganography (ADG)

Findings of ACL-IJCNLP 2021; arXiv author copy. Reviewed 2026-10-18.

An influential method that recursively groups candidate tokens to carry message bits. Useful for understanding why grouping methods can sacrifice capacity.

Later work qualifies exact distribution-preservation claims: arbitrary discrete probabilities cannot generally be partitioned into perfectly equal-probability groups.
