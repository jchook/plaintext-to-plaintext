   The research process itself posed limited direct risk. The

We did not collect personal data, involve human subjects,

Impact of the Publication The publication has both posi-
tive and negative implications. On the positive side, it gives
the community a more realistic framework for reasoning about
covert communication when receivers have partial, rather than
full or zero, channel knowledge. This can improve both sci-
entific understanding and defensive analysis by clarifying
what kinds of partial access may already be enough to en-
able nontrivial steganography. On the negative side, like most
steganography research, it could inform the design of more
practical covert channels.


Mitigation To mitigate this risk, we frame the contribution
as foundational analysis rather than a deployment recipe; we
explicitly state the assumptions and limitations of the weakly
asymmetric model so its practical utility is not overstated; and
we evaluate only controlled, public-resource instantiations.
