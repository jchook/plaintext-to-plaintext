# From Symmetry toward Weak Asymmetry: Secure Steganography under the Receiver's Partial Channel Knowledge

USENIX Security 2026. Reviewed 2026-10-18.

Allows receiver-side information such as token rankings instead of exact probability values. Directly relevant to numerical mismatches and eventual decoding across different devices.

Decoding still depends on the required partial information staying consistent. Precision-mismatch experiments are evidence for that setting, not proof of arbitrary phone-to-desktop compatibility.
