# OD-Stega: LLM-Based Relatively Secure Steganography via Optimized Distributions

EACL 2026. Reviewed 2026-10-18.

Optimizes the available token entropy subject to a KL or total-variation divergence budget. Addresses a separate bottleneck from coding efficiency and gives a principled alternative to choosing temperature by trial and error.

A nonzero divergence budget deliberately changes the original sampling distribution. Higher capacity must be evaluated against text quality and detectability.
