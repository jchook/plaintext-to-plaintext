# Neural Linguistic Steganography

EMNLP-IJCNLP 2019. Reviewed 2026-10-18.

Direct prior art for our pretrained language model plus arithmetic coding. Explains how secret bits drive token choices and how the receiver reconstructs those choices from shared probabilities.

A foundational construction, not evidence that our finite framing, candidate filtering, or numerical runtime preserves the original model distribution.
