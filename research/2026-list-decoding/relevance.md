# Provably Secure Steganography Based on List Decoding

April 2026 arXiv preprint, v3. Reviewed 2026-10-18.

Keeps candidate messages across tokens and resolves ambiguity with a suffix. Useful as an alternative way to approach the available entropy when instantaneous token choices carry little information.

Needs message length, can maintain up to 2^20 candidates, and allows a small failure probability. A research agent flagged a possible weighted-to-unweighted inequality issue in the capacity argument; this concern was not independently audited. Treat the bound as an author claim pending review.
