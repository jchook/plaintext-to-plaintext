# Linguistic Steganography via Self-Adjusting Asymmetric Number System

Computational Linguistics 2026. Reviewed 2026-10-18.

Another modern ANS-based approach, with adaptive candidate selection and user-selected embedding rates. Useful for comparing capacity, generation quality, and coding overhead.

Keep its rate and distortion definitions separate from claims of exact preservation of an unmodified model distribution. It is a distinct method from ANStega.
