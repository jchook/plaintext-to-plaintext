# HiTMS: A High-Throughput Multi-Stream Linguistic Steganography Framework

July 2026 arXiv preprint, v2. Reviewed 2026-10-18.

Adds framing and scheduling for multiple batched cover streams. Reports up to 4.3 times throughput. Its implementation includes Llama3.2-3B, making it relevant to our current model choice.

Throughput gains do not imply shorter total cover. Its protocol-utilization metric must not be confused with entropy utilization. The CUDA implementation and matching batch schedule are not evidence of mobile interoperability.
