# ReTokSync: Self-Synchronizing Tokenization Disambiguation for Generative Linguistic Steganography

April 2026 arXiv preprint. Reviewed 2026-10-18.

Resets generation state to the receiver's retokenized view when ambiguity occurs, limiting cascading errors. Promising for retaining capacity with little runtime overhead.

Above 99.7% accuracy refers to bits, not complete messages. Its evaluated 100% recovery uses additional correction covers through a reliable auxiliary channel. It is not yet our one-cover exact-recovery requirement.
