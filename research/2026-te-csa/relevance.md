# TE-CSA: trajectory entropy-aware cross-step aggregation for improving embedding capacity of distribution-preserving linguistic steganography

Cybersecurity, 13 October 2026. Reviewed 2026-10-18.

Recent diffusion-model extension that aggregates embedding opportunities across denoising steps. Reports 1.84 bits/token versus 0.09 for its architecture-matched timestep-local baseline.

Its preservation claim assumes a distribution-preserving underlying embedding mechanism. It is a different architecture from our autoregressive POC, and its relative gains cannot be applied to our byte expansion.
