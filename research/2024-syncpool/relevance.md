# Provably Secure Disambiguating Neural Linguistic Steganography (SyncPool)

IEEE TDSC, online 2024; often cited as 2025; arXiv author copy. Reviewed 2026-10-18.

Directly addresses our visible-text requirement. Groups prefix-ambiguous token pieces and uses synchronized cryptographic randomness within groups, preserving the token distribution while allowing decoding.

The within-group randomness carries no payload, so disambiguation can reduce usable capacity. Exact token synchronization still depends on matched inference and shared state.
