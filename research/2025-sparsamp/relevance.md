# SparSamp: Efficient Provably Secure Steganography Based on Sparse Sampling

USENIX Security 2025. Reviewed 2026-10-18.

Strong modern baseline combining distribution preservation with low sampling overhead. In the RRC paper's matched Llama-2-7B comparison it reaches 3.38 bits/token and is faster than RRC.

Headline token rates do not include a universal solution to tokenization ambiguity. Compare identical distributions and full transmitted bytes before inferring gains for our POC.
