# STEAD: Robust Provably Secure Linguistic Steganography with Diffusion Language Model

NeurIPS 2025. Reviewed 2026-10-18.

Explores resistance to token insertion, deletion, and substitution using diffusion language models and error correction. Relevant if the channel must tolerate edited cover text.

A different model architecture and channel objective from our autoregressive Llama POC. Robustness claims are limited to the evaluated attacks and settings.
