# A Comprehensive Survey on Linguistic Steganography: Methods, Countermeasures, Evaluation, and Challenges

August 2026 arXiv survey; authors report EMNLP 2026 acceptance. Reviewed 2026-10-18.

Map of the field, including 148 steganographic methods, tokenization fixes, evaluation metrics, and engineering limitations. Useful starting point and bibliography for continuing this research.

A survey indexes claims rather than independently validating every method. Return to the primary papers for security assumptions, exact recovery requirements, and comparable capacity measurements.
