# Computing Low-Entropy Couplings for Large-Support Distributions (ARIMEC)

UAI 2024. Reviewed 2026-10-18.

Extends practical minimum-entropy coupling to large, autoregressive distributions. Relevant when investigating structured message priors and information-efficient encoding.

A research alternative with different computation and message-distribution assumptions. It is not a replacement for authenticated encryption.
