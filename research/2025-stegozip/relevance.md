# StegoZip: Enhancing Linguistic Steganography Payload in Practice with Large Language Models

NeurIPS 2025. Reviewed 2026-10-18.

Targets our expansion problem by compressing the secret before embedding. Reports about 2.5 times the payload using semantic pruning, rank-based compression, and explicit corrections for restoration errors.

Uses a trained restoration model; main experiments involve larger models, with a 3B ablation. Reported semantic fidelity metrics do not replace our requirement to check exact bytes, Unicode, and whitespace.
