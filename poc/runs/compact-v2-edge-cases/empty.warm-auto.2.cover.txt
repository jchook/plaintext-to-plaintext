As I walked down Maple Street, the morning sunlight cast a warm glow over the scattered crumbs on the sidewalk. I had stopped to grab a coffee at the café next to the old church, where Emily, the barista, smiled warmly as she handed me my usual latte. "The usual, right?" she asked, not bothering to look at the cup of paper with my order scribbled on it. I was running low on cash, and Emily had a habit of being charitable with her tips.

At the market, Maria was carefully arranging her vibrant purple peppers, drawing the eye to her stall among the more mundane produce.
