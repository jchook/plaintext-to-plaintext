Today was one of those mornings where everything feels just right. woke up a bit earlier than usual, mostly because the light coming through the window seemed brighter and the air felt crisper than usual. headed downstairs to start the coffee maker, which has gotten to the point where it starts to make a nice gurgling sound every few minutes to let me know it's working. poured myself a cup of the dark roast, added a bit of cream, and sat down at the table to watch the sun rise a bit more.

After a while, I decided to take a walk through town. nothing specific to do, just a nice day and the urge to get out. the streets were quiet, as they often are on weekdays, but the birds seemed particularly loud, like they were trying to make up for the lack of human activity. stopped at the corner of my street and the park to take a quick walk through the gardens. it's always a pleasure to see what's in bloom – today it was a riot of color from the bulbs I'd planted last fall. smelled the roses, of course, and gave a quick nod to the woman watering her petunias over by the fence.

After my walk, I had a few errands to run. picked up a loaf of bread from the bakery down the street – still uses that old-fashioned stone oven, which gives the bread a wonderful chewiness – and stopped by the post office to mail a package to a friend who's been having a bit of a rough time lately. paid for it at the window, and our conversation was pleasant but brief, since we were both short on time. on the whole, it was nice to be of a service.

I was meant to meet a friend for brunch, but things got a bit complicated with the timing. ended up pushing it back an hour, and by the time we met up, it was late morning already. we chose a spot that's become a bit of a favorite of ours over the years – a cozy little café with a view of the river that's always enjoyable. we ordered a couple of sandwiches and some fruit, and spent the next hour catching up on each other's lives.
